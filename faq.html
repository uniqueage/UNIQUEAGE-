Which sizes of dishwash do you sell?
Every dishwash comes in 500ml, 750ml, 1L and 2L sizes. Prices scale with size — the 2L jug is the best value per litre for busy kitchens. Perfumes come in 30ml, 50ml and 100ml.
How long does delivery take?
Nationwide delivery takes 2–4 business days. Orders placed before 2pm ship the same day. You'll get a call from the dispatch team to confirm your address.
When is delivery free?
Delivery is free on every order over ₦10,000. Orders below that pay a flat ₦1,500. Tip: the 2L dishwash jugs and multi-item bundles cross the free-delivery line quickly.
How do I get the 10% discount?
Use the promo code SPARKLE10 on the cart page for 10% off your order subtotal. It stacks with free delivery.
What payment methods do you accept?
Pay on delivery (cash or transfer), bank transfer before dispatch, or card at checkout. This demo store doesn't take real payments — no money moves.
Can I return a product?
Yes — every order is covered by our 30-day guarantee. If a product arrives damaged or isn't what you expected, contact us within 30 days for a refund or replacement.
Do you sell wholesale or bulk?
Yes! We supply hotels, restaurants, salons and retailers. Reach out through the contact page for trade pricing on cases and pallets.
Is my cart saved between visits?
Yes — your cart is stored in this browser, so it stays until you check out or remove the items. Signing up lets you track your order history too.
